# Day 7 — Block 1 Rubric

Programme-standard weights, expanded into Block 1–specific observable criteria. This rubric scores **only** the Block 1 graded practical (`ASSESSMENT.md`) — the notification-escaping fix, the hardened `.claude/settings.json`, and the two `.claude/rules/` files. **The capstone (Blocks 2–3) has its own rubric in `capstone/CAPSTONE.md` and is scored separately** — nothing here applies to it.

`scripts/grade.mjs` scores everything except Presentation (marked by hand from the trainer's walkthrough with the learner) — it prints a total out of 85 and Presentation is added on top by the trainer, out of 15, for 100 overall.

| Criterion | Weight | Full marks | Partial marks | Zero marks |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Functionality** | 30 | Both `buildTaskAssignedNotification` and `buildTaskCompletedNotification` escape every user-supplied value (`task.title`, `task.description`, `user.name`) before interpolation; a crafted `<script>`-shaped input, checked independently by the grader, is escaped rather than passed through raw, and the check correctly fails when re-run against a known-vulnerable version of the file (anti-cheat revert-check passes); plain non-malicious text still renders unmangled; `npm test` is fully green with no regressions. | One value or one function still unescaped (e.g. `description` fixed, `user.name` missed); escaping is over-aggressive and corrupts plain text; the fix works but introduces an unrelated test failure elsewhere. | `notifyService.ts` still interpolates raw values into HTML; the grader's independent crafted-input check fails against the submitted source. |
| **Tool usage** | 25 | A committed regression test under `tests/` exercises an HTML-injection-shaped input and passes, **and** demonstrably fails when the fix is reverted (grader's own anti-cheat check on the learner's test); `.claude/rules/security.md` and `.claude/rules/testing.md` both exist with substantive, specific content covering injection prevention, ownership checks, secret handling, and the regression-test-must-fail-when-reverted discipline. | Regression test exists and passes but doesn't clearly discriminate fixed from unfixed code when the grader reverts the source; one of the two rules files is present but thin (a stub, or generic boilerplate not specific to this codebase). | No regression test added under `tests/` beyond what shipped at the start of the day; `.claude/rules/` doesn't exist at all. |
| **Code quality** | 20 | `.claude/settings.json`'s `deny` list is meaningfully extended past the one-entry starting point and specifically covers secret/credential file reads; its `ask` list is meaningfully extended past the one-entry starting point and specifically covers risky-but-sometimes-legitimate operations (install/publish, writes to `package.json` or `.github/**`); the file remains valid JSON. | Only one of `deny`/`ask` meaningfully extended; entries added but too broad to be useful (e.g. a wildcard that defeats the purpose of scoping) or too narrow to cover the stated risk categories. | `.claude/settings.json` unchanged from its Day 7 starting state, or edited into invalid JSON. |
| **Presentation** | 15 (hand-scored) | Learner can explain, specifically, why all three interpolated values needed escaping (not just the one they noticed first), why a regression test that doesn't fail on revert isn't a real test, and the reasoning behind each `deny`/`ask` placement they chose — not generic security platitudes. | Can explain the fix in general terms but not the specific "all three values, both functions" pattern; can describe what was added to `.claude/settings.json` but not why a given rule landed in `deny` versus `ask`. | Cannot walk through what was changed or why; explanation reads as guesswork rather than understanding of the fix. |
| **Best practices** | 10 | The pre-existing `hooks` block in `.claude/settings.json` is untouched; the original `deny` protection (`Bash(rm:*)`) is still present after hardening, not accidentally dropped; no files outside the scope of this task (`notifyService.ts`, its test, `.claude/settings.json`, `.claude/rules/*`) were touched. | A pre-existing protection was narrowed or removed while adding new ones, without acknowledgment; a small unrelated file was touched incidentally. | Hooks wiring removed or broken; the original `rm:*` deny rule dropped; unrelated files changed with no explanation. |

**Scoring note for the trainer**: `scripts/grade.mjs` prints a per-criterion table with points earned out of 30/25/20/10 (Functionality, Tool usage, Code quality, Best practices — Presentation is excluded from its total) and a combined total out of 85. Add your hand-scored Presentation mark (0–15) to get the final mark out of 100. The programme pass mark is 60/100 overall; the script itself exits non-zero below 60/85 on its own scope as an early warning, but the authoritative pass/fail decision is the full 100-point total including your Presentation mark.

**Validated against the starting state**: this grader is checked to score low and exit non-zero against Day 7's unmodified starting state (`notifyService.ts` still unescaped, `.claude/settings.json` still at its thin baseline, no `.claude/rules/` directory) — if it doesn't, the grader itself is broken and its score should not be trusted.
